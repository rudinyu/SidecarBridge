# macOS 1.4 (113): Viewer reconnect video recovery

## Fix

- Viewer reconnect recovery no longer cancels an already-running
  ScreenCaptureKit rebuild when `viewer-foreground` and `startFallback` arrive
  close together.
- If the Host still reports an active stream but its `SCStream` source has
  disappeared, resume now creates a fresh capture source instead of only
  requesting a keyframe.
- A transient capture rebuild failure retries automatically while the
  authenticated Viewer connection remains active. Pairing credentials and
  saved routes are not cleared as part of video recovery.
